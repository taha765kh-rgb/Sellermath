Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Welcome to SellerMath. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is handled when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

SellerMath provides browser-based calculators. The calculator values you enter are processed in your browser by the website's JavaScript code. Our current calculator code does not send these values to a SellerMath server or save them in a SellerMath account.

Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs as part of providing website hosting. Please refer to GitHub's own privacy information for details.

2. Cookies and Tracking

The current SellerMath website does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics tracking scripts, or account-tracking technology. This may change if analytics, advertising or other services are added in the future.

Third-party hosting or services may have their own technical data practices.

3. How We Use Information

Calculator inputs are used to calculate results in your browser. We do not currently operate user accounts or a database of calculator submissions.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. If we add advertising, analytics, affiliate links or other third-party services, those providers may collect information under their own privacy policies. We will update this policy when our practices change.

5. Children's Privacy

SellerMath is a general business calculator website and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

6. Your Choices

You can choose what values you enter into our calculators. Avoid entering confidential or sensitive information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website develops. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the SellerMath website operator through the contact information published on the website when available.
